Unit 1: Introduction to Statistics

Nature of Statistics

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Nature of Statistics, part of Unit 1: Introduction to Statistics. What statistics is, the kinds of data, how samples are chosen, and how data are displayed.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Nature of Statistics in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

Statistics is the science of collecting, organizing, analyzing and interpreting data. Descriptive statistics summarize a data set; inferential statistics generalize from a sample to a population.

Formula

$$\text{sample statistic}\approx\text{population parameter}$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. A survey of 50 students finds 30 own laptops
  2. Descriptive: 30/50 = 60% own one
  3. Inferential: estimate that about 60% of all students own one

Real-Life Applications

Surveys, quality checks and dashboards all start with well-collected, well-presented data.
