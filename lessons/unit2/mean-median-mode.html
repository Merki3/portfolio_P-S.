Unit 2: Measures of Central Tendency and Dispersion

Mean, Median, and Mode

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Mean, Median, and Mode, part of Unit 2: Measures of Central Tendency and Dispersion. Summarizing a data set by its center and by how spread out it is.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Mean, Median, and Mode in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

The mean is the average, the median is the middle value of ordered data, and the mode is the most frequent value.

Formula

$$\bar{x}=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i}{n}$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. Data: 2, 4, 4, 6, 9
  2. Mean = 25/5 = 5
  3. Median = 4 (middle value)
  4. Mode = 4 (appears twice)

Real-Life Applications

Comparing server response times, exam scores or sensor readings with one or two numbers.

Practice Quiz

What is the median of 3, 7, 9, 12?
