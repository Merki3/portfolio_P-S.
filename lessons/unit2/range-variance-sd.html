Unit 2: Measures of Central Tendency and Dispersion

Range, Variance, and Standard Deviation

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Range, Variance, and Standard Deviation, part of Unit 2: Measures of Central Tendency and Dispersion. Summarizing a data set by its center and by how spread out it is.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Range, Variance, and Standard Deviation in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

Range is the maximum minus the minimum. Variance averages the squared deviations from the mean, and the standard deviation is its square root, in the original units.

Formula

$$s^2=\frac{\sum (x_i-\bar{x})^2}{n-1},\qquad s=\sqrt{s^2}$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. Data: 2, 4, 6 so the mean is 4
  2. Squared deviations: 4, 0, 4 which sum to 8
  3. s² = 8/(3 − 1) = 4
  4. s = √4 = 2

Real-Life Applications

Comparing server response times, exam scores or sensor readings with one or two numbers.
