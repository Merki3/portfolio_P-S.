Unit 3: Probability

Basic Concepts

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Basic Concepts, part of Unit 3: Probability. Measuring chance with basic rules and counting techniques.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Basic Concepts in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

An experiment produces outcomes that form a sample space S, and an event is a subset of S. For equally likely outcomes, probability is favorable outcomes divided by total outcomes.

Formula

$$P(A)=\frac{n(A)}{n(S)}$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. Roll a fair die
  2. Event A = even number = {2, 4, 6}
  3. n(A) = 3 and n(S) = 6
  4. P(A) = 3/6 = 1/2

Real-Life Applications

Spam filters, risk assessment and counting possible passwords all rely on probability.
