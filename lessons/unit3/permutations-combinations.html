Unit 3: Probability

Permutations and Combinations

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Permutations and Combinations, part of Unit 3: Probability. Measuring chance with basic rules and counting techniques.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Permutations and Combinations in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

Permutations count ordered arrangements; combinations count selections where order does not matter.

Formula

$${}_nP_r=\frac{n!}{(n-r)!},\qquad {}_nC_r=\frac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!}$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. Choose 3 of 5 students: C(5,3) = 5!/(3!·2!) = 10
  2. Rank 3 of the 5 in order: P(5,3) = 5 × 4 × 3 = 60

Real-Life Applications

Spam filters, risk assessment and counting possible passwords all rely on probability.

Practice Quiz
