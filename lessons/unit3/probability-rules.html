Unit 3: Probability

Probability Rules

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Probability Rules, part of Unit 3: Probability. Measuring chance with basic rules and counting techniques.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Probability Rules in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

The complement rule is P(not A) = 1 − P(A). The addition rule is P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A and B). For independent events, P(A and B) = P(A)P(B).

Formula

$$P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. P(A) = 0.5, P(B) = 0.4, P(A and B) = 0.2
  2. P(A or B) = 0.5 + 0.4 − 0.2
  3. P(A or B) = 0.7

Real-Life Applications

Spam filters, risk assessment and counting possible passwords all rely on probability.

Practice Quiz

Independent events have P = 0.5 and P = 0.3. What is P(both)?
