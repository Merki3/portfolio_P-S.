Unit 5: Normal Distribution

Applications of the Normal Distribution

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Applications of the Normal Distribution, part of Unit 5: Normal Distribution. The bell curve, z-scores and the standard normal table.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Applications of the Normal Distribution in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

Standardizing lets us find proportions, percentiles and cut-off scores for any normally distributed measurement.

Formula

$$x=\mu+z\sigma$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. Exam: μ = 70, σ = 10
  2. Top 10% cut-off has z ≈ 1.28
  3. x = 70 + 1.28(10) = 82.8

Real-Life Applications

Grading on a curve, quality-control limits and percentile rankings.
