Unit 5: Normal Distribution

Characteristics of the Normal Distribution

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Characteristics of the Normal Distribution, part of Unit 5: Normal Distribution. The bell curve, z-scores and the standard normal table.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Characteristics of the Normal Distribution in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

The normal curve is bell-shaped and symmetric about the mean, so mean = median = mode. About 68%, 95% and 99.7% of values lie within 1, 2 and 3 standard deviations.

Formula

$$f(x)=\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}\,e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. Scores have μ = 70 and σ = 10
  2. Within 2 SD: 70 ± 2(10)
  3. About 95% of scores lie between 50 and 90

Real-Life Applications

Grading on a curve, quality-control limits and percentile rankings.

Practice Quiz

About what percent of values lie within 1 SD of the mean?
