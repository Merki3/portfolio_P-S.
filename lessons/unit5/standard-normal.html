Unit 5: Normal Distribution

Standard Normal Distribution (Z-distribution)

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Standard Normal Distribution (Z-distribution), part of Unit 5: Normal Distribution. The bell curve, z-scores and the standard normal table.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Standard Normal Distribution (Z-distribution) in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

The standard normal distribution is the normal distribution with mean 0 and standard deviation 1. Any normal value can be converted to it.

Formula

$$Z\sim N(0,1)$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. The curve is symmetric about 0
  2. P(Z < 0) = 0.5
  3. P(Z > 0) = 0.5

Real-Life Applications

Grading on a curve, quality-control limits and percentile rankings.
