Unit 5: Normal Distribution

Z-Scores and Standardization

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Z-Scores and Standardization, part of Unit 5: Normal Distribution. The bell curve, z-scores and the standard normal table.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Z-Scores and Standardization in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

A z-score tells how many standard deviations a value lies above or below the mean.

Formula

$$z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. Score x = 85, μ = 70, σ = 10
  2. z = (85 − 70)/10
  3. z = 1.5, so 1.5 SD above the mean

Real-Life Applications

Grading on a curve, quality-control limits and percentile rankings.
