Unit 6: Sampling Distribution and Central Limit Theorem

Population and Sample

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Population and Sample, part of Unit 6: Sampling Distribution and Central Limit Theorem. How sample means behave, and why the Central Limit Theorem matters.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Population and Sample in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

A population is the entire group of interest and a parameter describes it. A sample is a subset of it and a statistic describes the sample.

Formula

$$\mu\ \text{(parameter)},\qquad \bar{x}\ \text{(statistic)}$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. Mean age of all 12,000 students is μ (unknown)
  2. Sample 100 students and compute x̄
  3. x̄ is used to estimate μ

Real-Life Applications

Polling and A/B testing depend on how much sample means vary.
