Unit 6: Sampling Distribution and Central Limit Theorem

Applications of Sampling Distribution

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Applications of Sampling Distribution, part of Unit 6: Sampling Distribution and Central Limit Theorem. How sample means behave, and why the Central Limit Theorem matters.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Applications of Sampling Distribution in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

Sampling distributions let us find the probability that a sample mean falls in a given range. They are the basis of confidence intervals and tests.

Formula

$$z=\frac{\bar{x}-\mu}{\sigma/\sqrt{n}}$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. μ = 100, σ = 15, n = 36
  2. SE = 15/6 = 2.5
  3. For x̄ = 104: z = 4/2.5 = 1.6

Real-Life Applications

Polling and A/B testing depend on how much sample means vary.
