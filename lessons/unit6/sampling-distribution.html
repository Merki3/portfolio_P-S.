Unit 6: Sampling Distribution and Central Limit Theorem

Sampling Distribution of the Sample Mean

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Sampling Distribution of the Sample Mean, part of Unit 6: Sampling Distribution and Central Limit Theorem. How sample means behave, and why the Central Limit Theorem matters.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Sampling Distribution of the Sample Mean in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

If we draw many samples of size n, the sample means form a distribution. It is centered at the population mean.

Formula

$$\mu_{\bar{x}}=\mu$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. Population mean μ = 50
  2. Average of all possible sample means = 50
  3. Individual x̄ values vary around 50

Real-Life Applications

Polling and A/B testing depend on how much sample means vary.
