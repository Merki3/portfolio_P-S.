Margin of Error

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Margin of Error, part of Unit 7: Estimation and Hypothesis Testing. Estimating parameters and testing claims with sample data.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Margin of Error in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

The margin of error is half the width of a confidence interval. It gets smaller with a larger n or a lower confidence level.

Formula

$$E=z_{\alpha/2}\,\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. σ = 10, n = 100, 95% confidence
  2. E = 1.96 × 10/√100
  3. E = 1.96

Real-Life Applications

Deciding whether a new algorithm is really faster, or whether a treatment actually works.
