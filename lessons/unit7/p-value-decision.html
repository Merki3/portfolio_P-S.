Unit 7: Estimation and Hypothesis Testing

p-value and Decision Rule

Introduction

In this lesson you will study p-value and Decision Rule, part of Unit 7: Estimation and Hypothesis Testing. Estimating parameters and testing claims with sample data.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain p-value and Decision Rule in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

The p-value is the probability, assuming H₀ is true, of a result at least as extreme as the one observed. Reject H₀ when p ≤ α.

Formula

$$\text{Reject }H_0\ \text{if}\ p\le\alpha$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. z = 2.1 in a two-tailed test
  2. p = 2 × 0.0179 = 0.0358
  3. 0.0358 < 0.05, so reject H₀

Real-Life Applications

Deciding whether a new algorithm is really faster, or whether a treatment actually works.
