Unit 7: Estimation and Hypothesis Testing

Point Estimation

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Point Estimation, part of Unit 7: Estimation and Hypothesis Testing. Estimating parameters and testing claims with sample data.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Point Estimation in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

A point estimator is a single sample value used to estimate a parameter. The sample mean x̄ estimates μ, and the sample proportion estimates p.

Formula

$$\hat{p}=\frac{x}{n}$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. 42 of 120 sampled customers prefer online shopping
  2. p̂ = 42/120
  3. p̂ = 0.35

Real-Life Applications

Deciding whether a new algorithm is really faster, or whether a treatment actually works.
