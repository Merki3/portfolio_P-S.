Unit 7: Estimation and Hypothesis Testing

Level of Significance (α)

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Level of Significance (α), part of Unit 7: Estimation and Hypothesis Testing. Estimating parameters and testing claims with sample data.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Level of Significance (α) in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

The level of significance α is the probability of rejecting a true H₀ (a Type I error). Common choices are 0.10, 0.05 and 0.01.

Formula

$$\alpha=P(\text{reject }H_0\mid H_0\text{ is true})$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. Choose α = 0.05
  2. The risk of a false rejection is 5%
  3. Confidence level = 1 − α = 95%

Real-Life Applications

Deciding whether a new algorithm is really faster, or whether a treatment actually works.
