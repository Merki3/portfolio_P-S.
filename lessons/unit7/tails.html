Unit 7: Estimation and Hypothesis Testing

One-tailed and Two-tailed Tests

Introduction

In this lesson you will study One-tailed and Two-tailed Tests, part of Unit 7: Estimation and Hypothesis Testing. Estimating parameters and testing claims with sample data.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain One-tailed and Two-tailed Tests in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

Use a one-tailed test when the claim has a direction (> or <) and a two-tailed test when it says only that values differ (≠).

Formula

$$z=\frac{\bar{x}-\mu_0}{\sigma/\sqrt{n}}$$
