Unit 8: Correlation and Regression

Coefficient of Determination (r²)

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Coefficient of Determination (r²), part of Unit 8: Correlation and Regression. Measuring and modeling the relationship between two variables.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Coefficient of Determination (r²) in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

r² is the proportion of the variation in y that is explained by its linear relationship with x.

Formula

$$R^2=r^2$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. r = 0.8
  2. r² = 0.64
  3. 64% of the variation in y is explained by x

Real-Life Applications

Predicting sales, energy use or grades from related variables.
