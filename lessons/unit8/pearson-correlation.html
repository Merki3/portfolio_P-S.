Unit 8: Correlation and Regression

Pearson Correlation Coefficient

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Pearson Correlation Coefficient, part of Unit 8: Correlation and Regression. Measuring and modeling the relationship between two variables.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Pearson Correlation Coefficient in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

Pearson's r measures the strength and direction of a linear relationship, from −1 to +1.

Formula

$$r=\frac{n\sum xy-\sum x\sum y}{\sqrt{[n\sum x^2-(\sum x)^2][n\sum y^2-(\sum y)^2]}}$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. x = 1, 2, 3 and y = 2, 4, 6
  2. Σx = 6, Σy = 12, Σxy = 28, Σx² = 14, Σy² = 56
  3. Numerator = 3(28) − 6(12) = 12
  4. Denominator = √[(6)(24)] = 12
  5. r = +1

Real-Life Applications

Predicting sales, energy use or grades from related variables.
