Unit 8: Correlation and Regression

Regression Equation

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Regression Equation, part of Unit 8: Correlation and Regression. Measuring and modeling the relationship between two variables.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Regression Equation in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

The fitted equation ŷ = a + bx has an intercept a (the predicted y when x = 0) and a slope b (the change in ŷ for each 1-unit rise in x).

Formula

$$\hat{y}=a+bx$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. ŷ = 20 + 5x for study hours x
  2. Each extra hour adds 5 points
  3. At x = 0, ŷ = 20

Real-Life Applications

Predicting sales, energy use or grades from related variables.
