Unit 8: Correlation and Regression

Scatter Plot

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Scatter Plot, part of Unit 8: Correlation and Regression. Measuring and modeling the relationship between two variables.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Scatter Plot in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

A scatter plot graphs paired (x, y) data. Its pattern shows the direction, strength and form of a relationship.

Formula

$$(x_i,\,y_i),\quad i=1,2,\dots,n$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. Plot study hours (x) against exam score (y)
  2. Points rise from left to right
  3. This shows a positive relationship

Real-Life Applications

Predicting sales, energy use or grades from related variables.
