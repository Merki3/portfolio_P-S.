Unit 8: Correlation and Regression

Strength and Direction of Relationship

Introduction

In this lesson you will study Strength and Direction of Relationship, part of Unit 8: Correlation and Regression. Measuring and modeling the relationship between two variables.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain Strength and Direction of Relationship in your own words.
  • Use the key formula in a worked example.
  • Interpret the result in a real situation.

Explanation and Key Definitions

The sign of r gives the direction. The size |r| gives the strength: about 0.7 or more is strong, 0.3 to 0.7 is moderate, and below 0.3 is weak.

Formula

$$-1\le r\le 1$$

Worked Example

Step-by-step solution
  1. r = 0.82 is a strong positive relationship
  2. r = −0.35 is a moderate negative relationship
  3. r near 0 means no linear relationship

Real-Life Applications

Predicting sales, energy use or grades from related variables.

Practice Quiz
